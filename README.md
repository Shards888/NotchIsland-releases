# NotchIsland

A Dynamic Island for your Mac's notch, with a little pixel friend called Pip.

- **Music, weather and your day:** what's playing (with lyrics), a live sky, your next events and a timer.
- **Shelf and Stow:** drop files on the notch to keep, convert or share them.
- **Dictation:** hold right ⌥, talk, let go, and your words are typed for you. Runs entirely on your Mac.
- **Call notes:** record any call (no bot joins) and get a transcript, summary and to-dos.
- **Meetings:** Pip pops up before a Zoom, Meet or Teams call so you can join and take notes in one click.
- **Claude Code**, if you use it: see what Claude is doing and answer it from the notch.

Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no analytics.

## Install

1. Download **NotchIsland-x.y.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Shards888/NotchIsland-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **NotchIsland** into **Applications**.
3. Open NotchIsland from Applications. macOS will say it can't check it for malicious software. That's because it isn't from the App Store; click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to NotchIsland. Confirm with your password.

That's it: it now opens normally, starts by itself when you log in, and updates itself (Pip tells you when there's a new version). Hover over the notch to open it.

**You'll need** a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or newer. The call summary needs macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence; without it you still get the transcript.

macOS asks for each permission (microphone, calendar and so on) the first time a feature needs it, never up front.

## Remove

Quit NotchIsland from its menu (in the menu bar), then drag it from Applications to the Bin.
