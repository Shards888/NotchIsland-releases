<p align="center">A Dynamic Island for your Mac's notch, with a little pixel friend called Pip.</p>

## What it does

Hover over the notch and it opens. Click it to keep it open.

- **Your day at a glance:** what's playing (with lyrics), the weather over a live sky, your next events, and today's reminders.
- **⌥Space launcher:** open apps and files, do sums ("8 times 16"), search the web (`g`, `yt`, `maps`), find something you copied, or ask Pip a question. You can even hold right ⌥ and say it.
- **Reminders:** "remind me at 5 to call Mum" is saved to Reminders, so it's on your iPhone too, and it pops out of the notch when it's due.
- **Focus sessions:** a little ring counts down beside the notch, Pip goes quiet, and YouTube and friends can be nudged or blocked until you're done.
- **Dictation:** hold right ⌥, talk, let go, and your words are typed for you. Entirely on your Mac.
- **Shelf:** drop files on the notch to keep them handy, convert them, or AirDrop them.
- **Call notes:** record any call (no bot joins) and get a transcript, summary and to-dos.
- **Keep awake:** stop your Mac from sleeping for an hour, two, or until you say so.
- **Pip** lives beside the notch. It sleeps, follows your pointer, runs about, skids, and gets dizzy if you poke it too much.

Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no analytics.

## Install

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Shards888/NotchIsland-releases/main/installer.png" width="560" alt="The installer: drag NotchIsland into Applications"></p>

1. Download **NotchIsland-1.0.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Shards888/NotchIsland-releases/releases/latest), and open it.
2. Drag **NotchIsland** onto **Applications**.
3. Open **NotchIsland** from Applications.

**The first time only:** macOS says it can't check NotchIsland, because it isn't from the App Store.

1. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to NotchIsland. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

That's it. From then on it opens normally, starts by itself when you log in, and updates itself (Pip tells you when there's something new).

macOS asks for each permission (calendar, microphone, and so on) the first time a feature needs it, never all at once. Say no to anything you don't want; the rest still works.

The call summary needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence; without it you still get the transcript.

## Remove

Quit NotchIsland from its menu (the capsule icon in the menu bar), then drag it from Applications to the Bin.
